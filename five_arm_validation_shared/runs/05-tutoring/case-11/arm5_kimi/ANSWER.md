# Case 11 Answer

## 1. The misconception

The student has **swapped photosynthesis with cellular respiration** — they reversed the inputs and outputs. The root misconception is the belief that "everything alive breathes in oxygen and breathes out carbon dioxide," i.e., that plants get energy the same way animals do and that leaves work like lungs. This leads them to write the equation backwards (6O₂ + light → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O), which is also chemically nonsensical: it uses light to turn oxygen into carbon dioxide and water, with no carbon source for the CO₂.

## 2. The correct concept

**Correct answer:** The reactants (inputs) of photosynthesis are **carbon dioxide and water** (with light energy powering the process), and the products (outputs) are **glucose and oxygen**:

$$6CO_2 + 6H_2O \xrightarrow{\text{light}} C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6O_2$$

**Explanation for the student:**

Your reasoning mixes up two opposite processes. It's true that plants, like all living things, do **cellular respiration** — breaking down glucose using oxygen to release energy (glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water). But **photosynthesis** is a different, opposite job: it's how the plant *makes* its food in the first place.

Think of it this way: animals must eat to get glucose, but plants can't eat — they *build* glucose out of thin air and water. Using energy from sunlight, the plant takes in **carbon dioxide** from the air (through tiny pores in its leaves) and **water** from the soil (through its roots), and stitches them together into **glucose** (its food). **Oxygen** is left over and released as a waste product — which is why we say plants "give off oxygen."

Two ways to check yourself next time:
- **Follow the carbon:** a plant's body is built from carbon, and the only carbon source available is CO₂ from the air — so CO₂ must be an *input*, not an output.
- **Follow the energy:** light energy goes *in*, and it gets stored in the glucose that comes *out*. Your equation had light going in but no food being made.

So: photosynthesis = carbon dioxide + water → (with light) → glucose + oxygen. Respiration runs that equation in reverse, and plants do both — but photosynthesis is the one that makes the food and the oxygen.
