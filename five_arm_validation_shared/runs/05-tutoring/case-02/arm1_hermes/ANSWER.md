# Arm 1 — Reference answer — 05-tutoring case-02 (solve 2x + 3 = 11)

## 1. The misconception

The student treats the "=" sign as a command ("do something with these numbers") rather than a statement of **balance between two sides**. They saw "+3" and performed the operation that the symbol *suggests* (adding 3), instead of asking what operation would *undo* it. Underneath that is the deeper misconception: an equation is not a recipe to execute left-to-right; it's a scale that stays balanced only if you do the same thing to **both sides**.

## 2. Explaining it to the student

"An equation is a see-saw. On the left side sits a mystery box: **2x + 3**. On the right side sits **11**. The '=' means the two sides weigh exactly the same.

Your job isn't to follow the symbols like a recipe — it's to get the mystery box **alone** on its side, while keeping the see-saw balanced. And here's the rule that makes it work: **whatever you do to one side, you do to the other side too.**

Right now the box has a '+3' weighing it down. To get the box alone, we need to *remove* that 3. How do you undo adding 3? You **subtract 3**. (Notice: the *opposite* of what the symbol says — undo, not repeat.)

2x + 3 = 11 
2x + 3 − 3 = 11 − 3 ← subtract 3 from BOTH sides (see-saw stays level) 
2x = 8 

Now the box '2x' is alone: 2 of some number equals 8. To get ONE box, divide both sides by 2:

2x ÷ 2 = 8 ÷ 2 
**x = 4**

Now check it, because checking is how you *know*: put 4 back where x was. 2(4) + 3 = 8 + 3 = 11. ✓ It balances.

Here's why your way almost worked and didn't: you did 11 + 3 = 14, then ÷2 → 7. Put 7 back in the problem: 2(7) + 3 = 17, not 11. The check catches it every time.

**Rule to remember:** '=' is a balance, not a signal. Ask 'what does this symbol do to x?' — then *undo* it by doing the opposite operation to both sides.'"

## Quick check for the student (same idea, new problem)

*Solve 3x + 5 = 20.* 
Undo the +5: subtract 5 from both sides → 3x = 15. Undo the ×3: divide both sides by 3 → **x = 5**. Check: 3(5) + 5 = 20 ✓
