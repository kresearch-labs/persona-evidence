Ectopic ACTH-dependent Cushing syndrome (occult ectopic ACTH secretion, most likely from a small bronchial carcinoid tumor).

Justification: ACTH is elevated (106 pg/mL) with hypercortisolism and loss of diurnal rhythm, and cortisol fails to suppress even on high-dose (8 mg) dexamethasone with a normal pituitary MRI — indicating an ectopic, non-pituitary ACTH source; in a young heavy smoker with negative chest/abdominal CT this is classic for an occult bronchial carcinoid too small to image.
