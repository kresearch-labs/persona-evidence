# Answer

**Most likely diagnosis: Wernicke encephalopathy (thiamine/vitamin B1 deficiency) secondary to prolonged total parenteral nutrition without adequate thiamine supplementation.**

Justification: The patient on 6.5 months of TPN presents with the classic triad of altered mental status (somnolence), ocular motor dysfunction (uncoordinated eye movements, diplopia), and gait ataxia — with supportive fundoscopic findings (peripapillary hemorrhages, pale optic disc) and a normal CT — in a non-alcoholic patient whose only thiamine source was parenteral nutrition.
