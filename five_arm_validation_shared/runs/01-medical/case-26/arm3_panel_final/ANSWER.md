Erosive (inflammatory) osteoarthritis of the hands — generalized nodal osteoarthritis with active synovitis of the MCP joints and wrists, not rheumatoid arthritis.

Justification: post-menopausal woman with Heberden's and Bouchard's nodes, CMC-joint subluxation, hip/knee degenerative changes and a family history of osteoarthritis, plus ultrasound-proven osteophytes with power-Doppler synovitis at the 2nd/3rd MCPs but negative RF/anti-CCP, normal inflammatory markers and no chondrocalcinosis — which argues against rheumatoid arthritis and CPPD disease.
