# Case 29 — Answer

**Most likely diagnosis: Anti–glomerular basement membrane (anti-GBM) antibody disease (Goodpasture syndrome) — a pulmonary–renal syndrome with diffuse alveolar hemorrhage and rapidly progressive (crescentic) glomerulonephritis.**

*Why:* Hemoptysis with bilateral infiltrates and severe hypoxia (alveolar hemorrhage) occurring together with rapidly progressive glomerulonephritis — hematuria with RBC casts, 3+ proteinuria, and creatinine rising to 6.7 mg/dL over ~2 weeks — in a young woman is the classic presentation of anti-GBM (Goodpasture) disease.
