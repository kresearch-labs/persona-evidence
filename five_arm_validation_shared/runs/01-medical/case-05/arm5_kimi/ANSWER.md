Thyroid storm (from poorly controlled Graves' disease) occurring simultaneously with diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA) due to new-onset diabetes mellitus, both precipitated by influenza A infection.

Justification: severe hyperthyroid signs (goiter, exophthalmos, atrial fibrillation with HR 200, drowsiness, GI symptoms, infectious trigger — meeting criteria for thyroid storm) coexist with marked hyperglycemia (472 mg/dL), high anion-gap metabolic acidosis (pH 7.030, HCO3 3.4), and urinary ketones 4+ (DKA), with HbA1c 13.7% indicating preceding uncontrolled diabetes.
