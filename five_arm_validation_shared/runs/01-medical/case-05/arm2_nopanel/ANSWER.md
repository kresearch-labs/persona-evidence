Diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA) — the initial presentation of new-onset type 1 diabetes mellitus in a patient with Graves' disease, precipitated by the influenza A infection.

Why: severe hyperglycemia (472 mg/dL, HbA1c 13.7%), profound metabolic acidosis (pH 7.030, HCO3⁻ 3.4 mmol/L), 4+ ketonuria with Kussmaul respiration and drowsiness fulfill the DKA triad, while the diffuse goiter with exophthalmos on a poorly taken anti-thyroid drug identifies underlying Graves' disease — an autoimmune disorder strongly associated with type 1 diabetes.
