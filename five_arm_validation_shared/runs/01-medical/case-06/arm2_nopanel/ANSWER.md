Addison's disease (primary adrenal insufficiency), presenting here as an acute adrenal crisis.

Justification: Hyperpigmentation of the palms, soles, and mucous membranes with salt craving, postural hypotension, chronic abdominal pain/vomiting, and the typical electrolyte pattern of hyponatremia with hyperkalemia are the classic hallmarks of primary adrenal insufficiency.
