Neurosyphilis — the parenchymatous form: general paresis of the insane with tabetic features (taboparesis).

Justification: a 3-year progressive dementia–psychosis unresponsive to antipsychotics, with dysarthria, incontinence, bradykinesia, small pupils with impaired light reflexes, painless unhealed perforating (trophic) ulcers over pressure points, global cerebral–cerebellar atrophy, and CSF showing raised globulin (positive Pandy) with only 3 cells and normal glucose/protein is the classic late neurosyphilis (taboparesis) constellation.
