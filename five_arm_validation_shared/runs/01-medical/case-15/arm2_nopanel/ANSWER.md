# Case 15 — Answer

**Most likely diagnosis:** Guillain–Barré syndrome (GBS) — an acute inflammatory polyradiculoneuropathy, most consistent with the acute motor axonal neuropathy (AMAN) variant.

**One-line justification:** Antecedent watery diarrhea followed 10 days later by acute, symmetric, purely motor proximal-and-distal quadriparesis with abolished reflexes, no sensory or sphincter involvement, CSF albuminocytologic dissociation (protein 0.59 g/L with only 3 mononuclear cells/mm³), and neurogenic EMG changes with normal conduction velocities.
