# Answer

**Most likely diagnosis: Severe (critical) aortic stenosis.**

Justification: The new ejection systolic murmur radiating to the carotids, together with chest pain, fluid-refractory hypotension, and lateral ST depression (V4–V6) reflecting left ventricular strain/ischemia, points to critical aortic stenosis; the normal troponin, normal chest X-ray without mediastinal widening, and absent pulse/BP differentials argue against myocardial infarction and aortic dissection.
