Syndrome of inappropriate antidiuretic hormone secretion (SIADH), most likely paraneoplastic SIADH secondary to small cell lung cancer.

Justification: The patient has hypotonic hyponatremia (serum Na 111 mmol/L, serum osmolality 248 mosmol/kg) with inappropriately concentrated urine (urine osmolality 497 mosmol/kg) and elevated urine sodium (73 mmol/L) in a euvolemic patient with small cell lung cancer — the classic diagnostic picture of SIADH, refractory to fluid restriction.
