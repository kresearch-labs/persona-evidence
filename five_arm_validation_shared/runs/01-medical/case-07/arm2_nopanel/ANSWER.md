Syndrome of inappropriate antidiuretic hormone secretion (SIADH), paraneoplastic — secondary to small cell lung cancer.

Justification: Euvolemic hypotonic hyponatremia (Na 111–118 mmol/L, serum osmolality ~248–249) with inappropriately concentrated urine (urine osmolality 491–497) and elevated urine sodium (73–136 mmol/L) in a patient with small cell lung cancer, improving with fluid restriction, is the classic biochemical signature of SIADH.
