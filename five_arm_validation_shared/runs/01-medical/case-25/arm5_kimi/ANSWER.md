Wilson disease (hepatolenticular degeneration).

Justification: the combination of unexplained cirrhosis mislabelled as lean NAFLD, progressive neuropsychiatric symptoms (tremor, ataxia, dysarthria, dysphagia, depression), a golden-brown Kayser–Fleischer ring at the corneal margin, T2/FLAIR signal in the thalami/midbrain/pons, and normocytic (likely haemolytic) anaemia is classic for copper accumulation from Wilson disease.
