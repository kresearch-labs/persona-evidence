# Answer

**Most likely diagnosis: Phaeochromocytoma (a catecholamine-secreting adrenal tumour), presenting as a fatal catecholamine crisis precipitated by propranolol given without prior alpha-blockade.**

Justification: Years of episodic "anxiety" with paroxysmal hypertension (146/103 mmHg) in a young woman are classic for undiagnosed phaeochromocytoma, and unopposed alpha-adrenergic stimulation after beta-blockade with propranolol triggered the acute collapse, tachycardia (186 bpm), cyanosis and respiratory arrest.
