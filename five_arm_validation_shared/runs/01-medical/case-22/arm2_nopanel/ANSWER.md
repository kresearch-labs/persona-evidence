Massive (fatal) pulmonary embolism — acute pulmonary thromboembolism causing obstructive shock and respiratory arrest.

Justification: Sudden collapse with cyanosis, extreme tachycardia (186/min), unrecordable blood pressure and rapid respiratory arrest after weeks of unexplained cough and dyspnoea mislabelled as anxiety and a "chest infection" is the classic picture of massive PE, with beta-blocker toxicity (propranolol <0.1 mg/L), other drug causes (no toxicological history) and phaeochromocytoma (no documented palpitations, headache or episodic sweating) excluded.
