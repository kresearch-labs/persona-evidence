Sarcoidosis (granulomatous disease) causing calcitriol-mediated hypercalcemia, superimposed on chronic primary hyperparathyroidism.

Justification: hypercalcemia with an elevated 1,25-dihydroxyvitamin D (calcitriol 88, ref 18–72), normal PTHrP, and negative SPEP/UPEP indicates extrarenal calcitriol production by granulomas rather than humoral malignancy or myeloma, with the patient's underlying PHPT (PTH 253 vs baseline 160s) amplifying the calcium rise.
