Diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA) as the first presentation of previously undiagnosed diabetes mellitus, precipitated by COVID-19 (SARS-CoV-2 infection).

Justification: severe hyperglycemia (1507 mg/dL), metabolic acidosis (arterial pH 7.113), and ketonuria (2+) fulfill the DKA triad, while HbA1c of 10.1% indicates antecedent unrecognized diabetes and the positive antigen test with fever identifies COVID-19 as the likely precipitant (no pneumonia on CT and only mild inflammatory markers argue against sepsis as the primary cause).
