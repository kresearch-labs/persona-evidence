Diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA) — a hyperglycemic crisis in newly presenting diabetes mellitus (most consistent with fulminant/new-onset type 1 diabetes precipitated by the concurrent SARS-CoV-2 infection).

One-line justification: Marked hyperglycemia (1507 mg/dL) with ketonuria (2+) and severe metabolic acidosis (pH 7.113) causing altered consciousness is the diagnostic triad of DKA.
