Diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA) — the initial presentation of previously undiagnosed diabetes mellitus, precipitated by SARS-CoV-2 infection.

Justification: marked hyperglycemia (1507 mg/dL) with ketonuria (2+) and severe metabolic acidosis (pH 7.113) causing altered consciousness is the classic DKA triad, while HbA1c 10.1% indicates chronic pre-existing hyperglycemia and the absent pneumonia/mild inflammation argue against COVID-19 pneumonia or sepsis as the primary process.
