# Case 09 — Answer

**Most likely diagnosis: Listeria monocytogenes rhombencephalitis (listeric brainstem encephalitis).**

**One-line justification:** A prodromal flu-like illness followed by sequential cranial-nerve palsies (peripheral VII) with rapid brainstem deterioration, MRI foci confined to the brainstem, CSF showing high opening pressure, neutrophil-predominant pleocytosis, markedly elevated protein with normal glucose and negative Gram/India ink/acid-fast stains, plus worsening after corticosteroids in a middle-aged adult with prediabetes, is the textbook picture of Listeria rhombencephalitis.
