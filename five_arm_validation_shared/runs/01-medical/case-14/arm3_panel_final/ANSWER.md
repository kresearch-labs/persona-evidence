Myasthenia gravis — the bulbar (oropharyngeal) form, most likely acetylcholine-receptor antibody positive and thymoma-associated (the incidentally found anterior mediastinal mass).

Justification: fatigable weakness that worsens with exercise and improves with rest, affecting facial, masticatory, palatal and pharyngeal muscles with fatigable ptosis on sustained upgaze and shoulder abduction fatiguing in under 20 repetitions, together with an anterior mediastinal mass (thymoma), is the classic presentation of myasthenia gravis.
