Trousseau syndrome — cancer-associated hypercoagulability causing multiple cerebral infarctions (paraneoplastic thromboembolism, presumably from recurrent/occult mucin-producing carcinoma).

One-line justification: Multi-territory infarcts on DWI with markedly elevated D-dimer (28.2 µg/mL), no arrhythmia or cardiac thrombus, no large-artery occlusion, and no DIC — the classic pattern of cancer-related hypercoagulability (Trousseau syndrome), supported by strikingly high mucin-type tumor markers (CA19-9 5594 U/mL, CA125 277.3 U/mL).
