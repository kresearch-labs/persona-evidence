**Key point:** postop day-46 multiple brain infarcts with no cardiac/carotid source, soaring D-dimer, no DIC, and climbing tumor markers = recurrent-cancer hypercoagulability with embolic stroke — search for the recurrence, anticoagulate, and treat the tumor as the source.
