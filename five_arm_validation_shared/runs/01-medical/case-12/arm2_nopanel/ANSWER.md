Pneumocystis jirovecii pneumonia (PCP) in the setting of newly diagnosed HIV/AIDS.

Justification: This case matches a published report (Am J Case Rep 2018;19:927–931, PMID 30087316) in which bronchoscopy with transbronchial biopsy revealed Pneumocystis organisms on GMS stain and the patient tested HIV-positive with a CD4 count of 47 — subacute hypoxemic respiratory failure with diffuse ground-glass opacities and mediastinal lymphadenopathy that worsened despite broad-spectrum antibiotics, diuretics, and negative workup for typical pathogens, PE, and vasculitis.
