Most likely diagnosis: Cryptogenic organizing pneumonia (COP; formerly bronchiolitis obliterans organizing pneumonia, BOOP).

Justification: subacute dyspnea with hypoxemic respiratory failure, diffuse bilateral ground-glass opacities and infiltrates that mimic pneumonia/CHF, but with negative infectious workup (cultures, atypical titers), negative vasculitis/autoimmune serologies (ANCA, ANA), and failure to respond to broad-spectrum antibiotics — the classic picture of an organizing pneumonia rather than infection, pulmonary embolism, or decompensated heart failure.
