# Answer

**Most likely diagnosis: Infective endocarditis (subacute bacterial endocarditis) involving the tricuspid valve, complicating a congenital perimembranous ventricular septal defect.**

Justification: A child with a predisposing cardiac lesion (VSD) presents with a 2.5-month febrile illness, weight loss, hepatosplenomegaly, anemia, elevated inflammatory markers, and echocardiographic vegetations on the tricuspid valve (a major modified Duke criterion), with multiple dental caries as the likely portal of entry — fulfilling the picture of infective endocarditis.
