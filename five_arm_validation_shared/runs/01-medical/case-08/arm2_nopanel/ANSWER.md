Infective endocarditis (subacute bacterial endocarditis) of the tricuspid valve, complicating a congenital perimembranous ventricular septal defect.

Justification: Prolonged low-grade fever with weight loss, anemia, splenomegaly, and echocardiographic vegetations on the tricuspid valve in a child with a predisposing VSD and dental caries (a portal for viridans streptococci) satisfy the modified Duke criteria for infective endocarditis.
