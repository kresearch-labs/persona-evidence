Infective endocarditis (subacute bacterial endocarditis) of the tricuspid valve complicating a congenital perimembranous ventricular septal defect.

One-line justification: Prolonged low-grade fever with weight loss, anemia, splenomegaly and dental caries (portal of entry) in a child with a known VSD, plus echocardiographic tricuspid valve vegetations, fulfill the picture of subacute infective endocarditis.
