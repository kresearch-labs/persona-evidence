Most likely diagnosis: Spontaneous intracranial hypotension (SIH) due to a spinal cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) leak, precipitated by vigorous exertion.

Justification: An acute exertional-onset headache with band-like pain radiating from the neck down the spine, accompanied by tinnitus and phonophobia, is characteristic of CSF hypovolemia; SIH may be non-positional and brain/spinal MRI can be normal in a substantial minority of cases, so the unremarkable workup does not exclude it (confirmation would require CT/digital subtraction myelography to localize the leak).
