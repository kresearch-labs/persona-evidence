Osteomalacia (adult rickets) secondary to severe vitamin D deficiency — the constellation of diffuse bone pain, proximal myopathy, hypocalcemia, hypophosphatemia, markedly elevated alkaline phosphatase, 25-OH vitamin D of 10 ng/mL, and low bone mineral density is diagnostic of defective bone mineralization; the coexisting microcytic anemia suggests a malabsorptive cause (e.g., celiac disease) should be sought.
