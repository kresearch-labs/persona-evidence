Antiphospholipid syndrome (APS) — an acquired thrombophilia causing simultaneous venous and arterial thromboembolism, unmasked here by a month of apixaban nonadherence.

Why: prior "unprovoked" DVT/PE at age 44 plus acute multi-territory arterial thrombosis (proximal-mid LAD occlusion with anterior STEMI, distal aortic thrombus, popliteal occlusion), biventricular apical thrombi, and mild thrombocytopenia (114,000/μL) is the hallmark pattern of APS.
