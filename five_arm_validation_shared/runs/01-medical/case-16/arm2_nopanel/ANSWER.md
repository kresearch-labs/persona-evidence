**Cerebral venous sinus thrombosis (postpartum cerebral venous thrombosis).**

Justification: Severe progressive headache beginning in the third trimester, a similar postpartum headache with a previous pregnancy, multiparity, and seizures with new focal deficit and falling conscious level that persist despite full magnesium/hydralazine treatment on postpartum day 9 — with normal coagulation studies and D-dimer — are classic for cerebral venous sinus thrombosis mimicking refractory postpartum eclampsia.
