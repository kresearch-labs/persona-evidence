# Answer

**Most likely diagnosis: Cerebral venous sinus thrombosis (CVST / cerebral venous thrombosis).**

Justification: The puerperium is a profound hypercoagulable state, and the triad of severe postpartum headache, seizures refractory to magnesium sulfate, and a new focal neurological deficit (left-sided weakness) with declining consciousness — in the absence of any intracerebral abnormality explaining the picture — is classic for CVST masquerading as postpartum eclampsia; her recurrent postpartum headache in a previous pregnancy further supports a thrombotic rather than purely hypertensive etiology.
