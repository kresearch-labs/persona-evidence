The analysis concludes:
1. **Issues presented** — (a) the scope of the sole-discretion grant, (b) whether the implied covenant can supply an affirmative market-check obligation, and (c) the effect of the fiduciary-duty elimination (which under 6 Del. C. § 18-1101 cannot eliminate the implied covenant).
