Recommendation: Sell.

At roughly 75x estimated 2021 EV/EBITDA — about six times the packaged-food group's five-year average of 12–13x — Beyond Meat's price already discounts years of flawless execution that the current fundamentals do not support. The company is unprofitable; bulk-packaging promotions are compressing margins with only a promise that the drag eases; and the foodservice channel that carries the long-term growth story is suppressed, with major chains delaying plant-based rollouts. The one clear catalyst, a McDonald's McPlant partnership, rests on unconfirmed industry checks — and at today's multiple it is largely priced in already: if it proceeds, upside is limited; if it slips or fails, the multiple has far further to compress toward the 12–13x peer norm than to expand. The retail build-out (17,000 to ~28,000 doors) is real, but it is a food-company growth rate being paid for at tech-stock multiples, with pandemic timing uncertain in both channels. The risk/reward is asymmetric to the downside: sell, and revisit only on confirmed McPlant economics and a visible path to profitability.
