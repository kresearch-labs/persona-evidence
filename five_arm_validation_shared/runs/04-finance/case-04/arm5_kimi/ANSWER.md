# Case 04 — Tesla (as of April 11, 2018)

**Recommendation: Sell.**

Justification: At a $50.3 billion market cap — above Ford and nearing GM — Tesla was unprofitable and its entire valuation rested on flawless execution of the Model 3 ramp, which had just missed its own target (2,020 vs. 2,500 units/week), with deliveries flat quarter-over-quarter and an NTSB investigation adding regulatory/safety overhang, leaving the risk/reward at ~$320 skewed sharply to the downside.
