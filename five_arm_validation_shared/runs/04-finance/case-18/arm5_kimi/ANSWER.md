# Case 18 — Micron Technology (MU), September 12, 2018

**Recommendation: Sell.**

Justification: Micron's roughly 4–5x forward multiple was a classic cyclical-peak value trap — the "cheap" valuation rested on record earnings ($7.8B revenue, $3.15 EPS in FQ3 2018) that were about to collapse, since DRAM and NAND spot prices were already softening and customers were delaying procurement, a dynamic that historically snowballs into multi-quarter memory downturns far steeper than investors initially expect; the right move at the first confirmed sign of a downturn in a low-multiple memory leader is to exit, not hold through the cycle.
