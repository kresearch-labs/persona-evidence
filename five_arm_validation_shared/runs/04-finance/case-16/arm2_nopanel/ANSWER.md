Recommendation: Buy — sized for volatility.

Why: The August 23, 2023 report was not just a strong quarter but a wholesale reset of the earnings base. Data center revenue of ~$10B in a single quarter nearly matched the ~$15B of the entire prior fiscal year, October-quarter guidance of ~$16B was far above prior expectations, and the published trajectory — ~$41B of data center revenue for the fiscal year ending January 2024, ~$60B the next year, ~$100B by fiscal 2028 — implies several years of 30–50% growth at expanding, roughly 70%+ gross margins, with supply sold out and real pricing power. At the post-print valuation of just over $1 trillion, the stock traded around 40x the current fiscal year's earnings but only roughly 25–30x the following year's estimates — against 40–60% earnings growth, a multiple no richer than the market's own. The "easy money has been made" argument confuses the rally already delivered with the value still ahead: returns are set by estimate revisions, and those revisions were still moving up sharply. The genuine risks — extreme customer concentration among a handful of cloud buyers, China export controls, competition from AMD and customers' in-house silicon, and the inherent cyclicality of capex cycles — argue for careful position sizing and tolerance for drawdowns, not for refusing to own the cleanest proxy for a durable shift in enterprise spending.
