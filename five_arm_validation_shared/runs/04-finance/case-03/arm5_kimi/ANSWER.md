# Case 03 — Lehman Brothers, June 2008

**Recommendation: SELL**

Justification: As of early June 2008, Lehman faced a deteriorating outlook that had not yet hit reported results — S&P had just cut its rating to 'A' with a negative outlook, fiscal Q2 was expected to show meaningful deterioration from further mortgage-related write-downs, hedge basis risk, and de-levering, and its funding costs and counterparty relationships were hostage to a credit rating at risk of further downgrade; with the risk/reward skewed sharply to the downside and no near-term catalyst for recovery, the prudent action was to exit the position.
