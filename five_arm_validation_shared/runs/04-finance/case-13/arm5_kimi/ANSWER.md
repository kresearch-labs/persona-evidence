# Case 13 — Boeing (BA), April 8, 2019

**Recommendation: HOLD**

**Why:** Boeing's core franchise — a record $101.1B in 2018 revenue, a 5,500+ order backlog, and a duopoly position with Airbus in single-aisle jets — remains intact and argues against selling into the crisis, but the grounding's duration, delivery penalties, airline compensation claims, potential order cancellations, and long-term MAX reputational damage are all still unquantifiable, and with the stock still up ~21.5% YTD (richly valued despite the ~9% pullback), the risk/reward does not justify buying either; a client should hold and reassess once the grounding timeline and total costs become clearer.
