# Case 08 — Peloton Interactive (PTON), May 2022

**Recommendation: SELL**

**Justification (one line):** The entire bull case hangs on a single unproven assumption — that management can hit positive free cash flow in FY23 while growing members ~14x — yet the evidence at the date shows the opposite: a $757M quarterly loss, collapsing post-lockdown demand, halted production, excess inventory, an untested price cut/fee hike, and intensifying competition from Apple, Lululemon, Tonal, and reopened gyms, so even after a 90%+ decline the risk/reward favors selling rather than underwriting an unproven turnaround.
