**Recommendation: Hold.**

**One-line justification:** At $132.60, Zoom trades at ~38× its own FY23 adjusted-EPS guidance ($3.45–3.51, midpoint ~$3.48) for a business guiding to ~10% revenue growth and a ~31% EPS decline — no longer priced for perfection after a 66% de-rating, but not cheap enough to buy until management's load-bearing assumption (enterprise growing substantially faster than online, with growth re-accelerating late in the year) shows up in reported results.

**Why:** The Q4 beat (adjusted EPS $1.29 vs ~$1.07 consensus; revenue $1.07B vs ~$1.05B) and the $1B buyback confirm a still highly profitable franchise, and Zoom's habit of conservative guidance — this quarter's beat being the latest example — gives the deliberately low FY23 bar upside optionality. But the guide itself says the pandemic boom is ending: EPS falling from $5.07 to ~$3.48 on heavy platform investment, and Q1 guided below Street on both lines. Microsoft Teams competition and the unproven persistence of pandemic-era usage and free-user conversion put both the ~10% growth assumption and the multiple at risk. If enterprise growth disappoints, ~38× compresses toward the mid-20s (high-$80s stock); if it delivers, EPS recovering toward ~$5 in FY24 on a ~30× multiple supports $150+. Risk/reward is roughly balanced at $132.60 — hold, and upgrade only on evidence the enterprise mix-shift is real.
