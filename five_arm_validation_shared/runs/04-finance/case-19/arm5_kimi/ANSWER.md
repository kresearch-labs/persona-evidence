# Case 19 — Intel (INTC), July 24, 2020

**Recommendation: SELL.**

Justification: the 7nm delay breaks the one assumption Intel's value hangs on — that its in-house process technology would remain competitive — because with AMD already shipping TSMC-made 7nm server chips and no Intel fix before late 2022/2023, share loss and margin erosion are structural rather than cyclical, making the post-drop ~9–10x earnings multiple a value trap despite the strong $19.7B June quarter, ~2.5% dividend, and buybacks; the call fails only if Intel credibly restores its process cadence (or commits decisively to TSMC outsourcing) within about two years, which as of that date was unproven.
