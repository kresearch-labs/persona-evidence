# Case 19 — Intel (INTC), as of July 24, 2020

**Recommendation: HOLD.**

**Why (one line):** The 17% one-day selloff over-discounts a problem — 7nm slipping to late 2022/early 2023 — that leaves near-term economics intact (Q2 revenue $19.7B, +20% y/y, a profitable, cash-generative franchise with dominant server share, ~10x earnings and a ~3% dividend yield), so panic-selling is value-destructive; but a decade of process stumbles, AMD's structural 7nm share gains and a multiple capped for the next two to three years argue against committing new money until Intel resolves the existential in-house-vs-outsourced-foundry question.
