# Case 06 — Meta Platforms (META), October 26, 2022

**Recommendation: HOLD** (do not add at $97.94; only investors with high risk tolerance should keep full positions, and new money should wait).

**Why:**
- The bad news is largely priced in: at $97.94 the stock sits at 2016 levels, roughly one-fifth of its 2021 peak and down 61%+ YTD, trading at a low-teens multiple of even this year's depressed earnings — selling here locks in the collapse of a core advertising franchise that still generated $27.7B of revenue in a single quarter and funds everything else.
- But there is no catalyst to buy yet: revenue is falling (second consecutive decline, −4% YoY), net profit halved (−52% to $4.4B), Q4 guidance of $30–32.5B came in below the Street's ~$32.2B, and the headwinds (Apple's iOS privacy changes, TikTok share gains, macro ad pullback) are structural or unresolved, not clearly transient.
- The decisive risk is capital allocation: management is committed to continued billions in metaverse spending (Reality Labs lost $9.4B in nine months) plus an AI ramp, with no signaled discipline, so the "E" in the P/E can keep shrinking — the classic value-trap setup.
- Trigger to upgrade to Buy: evidence of cost discipline (metaverse losses capped or cut) or stabilization in ad revenue. Trigger to downgrade to Sell: another guidance cut with Reality Labs losses still widening.

**One-line justification:** With the ad-driven earnings base shrinking and metaverse losses uncapped there is no reason to add, but at a low-teens multiple of depressed earnings the stock already discounts the deterioration, so holding — not chasing or capitulating — is the risk-appropriate call as of that date.
