Recommendation: **Hold**.

Why: As known in late July 2022, the near-term picture is genuinely deteriorating — Q2 revenue of ~$1.30B (+16% y/y) missed estimates, the company posted an unexpected ~$1.2B net loss (driven largely by a ~$1.0B unrealized loss on its Global-E stake), GMV growth slowed to ~11%, gross margin compressed (logistics buildout incl. the $2.1B Deliverr deal), and management itself said it has no visibility into when e-commerce growth normalizes, with consumers shifting from goods to services — so there is no data yet to underwrite a reacceleration date, while the stock, even after an ~80% drawdown, still traded at a premium sales multiple with limited margin of safety. But it is not a Sell: Shopify retains a fortress balance sheet (~$5B cash), sticky merchant base, rising take rate (Payments/POS), and guided Q2 as its lowest-growth quarter of 2022 with sequential acceleration expected, and the 10% layoff shows management pivoting to efficiency — so the right call is to hold existing positions and wait for GMV growth to stabilize before adding.
