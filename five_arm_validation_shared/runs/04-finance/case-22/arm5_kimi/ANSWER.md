Recommendation: HOLD Shopify (SHOP) as of late July 2022.

Justification: The sharp share-price decline over the prior year has already reset much of the pandemic pull-forward in expectations, but with an unexpected quarterly loss, a revenue miss, inflation/FX headwinds, pressured merchant and GMV trends, and management admitting it has no visibility on when growth stabilizes, there is no fundamental catalyst to justify buying yet — while the 10% workforce reduction and new efficiency focus suggest the worst of the cost reset is being addressed, making a sell premature.
