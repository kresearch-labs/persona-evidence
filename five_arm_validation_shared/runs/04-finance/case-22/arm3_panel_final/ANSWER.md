Hold — as known in late July 2022, Shopify gave investors no near-term reason to buy: an unexpected quarterly loss, revenue below expectations, management warning that inflation, unfavorable currency moves and tough pandemic-era comparisons would keep pressuring merchandise volumes and merchant additions, and an admission it has no visibility into when e-commerce growth normalizes — capped by a ~10% layoff (~1,000 staff) that marks a pivot from growth-at-all-costs to efficiency. But with shares already down sharply from their pandemic highs, much of this deterioration was already priced in, so selling locks in the drawdown just as the valuation risk has shrunk. Hold until there is evidence that consumer spending and e-commerce growth rates are stabilizing.
