# Case 14 — General Electric (April 8, 2019)

**Recommendation: SELL**

Justification: The ~38% YTD rally to ~$10 prices in a recovery that the numbers do not yet show — industrial free cash flow is deeply negative (a projected $2.4–2.5B Q1 outflow and up to $2B of cash burn for 2019), Power FCF is not expected positive until 2021, GE Capital will keep consuming cash against unresolved insurance liabilities, and even the crown-jewel aviation business looks weaker than it appears — so the risk/reward favors selling into the optimism rather than paying for a turnaround that remains unproven.
