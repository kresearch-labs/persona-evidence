**Recommendation: Hold** — and for a new client, do not initiate at ~$19; accumulate only on a pullback toward the mid-$16s or on hard evidence that the server ramp is landing.

The competitive opening is real. Intel's 10nm slip to holiday 2019 hands AMD a genuine 7nm window on TSMC, and the June quarter — $1.76B revenue, +53% YoY, ~37% gross margin — confirms Ryzen and EPYC traction rather than mere narrative. The problem is not the story; it is the price. After an 85.8% YTD run, AMD traded around 40x 2018 and roughly 28x 2019 non-GAAP EPS on the estimates then in the market (~$0.45 and ~$0.65–0.70). That multiple already assumes the base case executes: server share roughly doubling from ~2% to ~5% in 2019 and climbing toward 9–10% by 2020, with gross margin expanding — not merely holding — as EPYC mixes in.

The entire call hangs on one assumption: the server-share ramp converting into revenue at rising gross margin. That is where the economics live (server CPUs carry the TAM and the margin), it is where AMD is weakest today (~2% share), and it is where Intel defends hardest — socket relationships, ecosystem lock-in, and a long history of meeting share gains with price cuts. If the ramp lands on schedule, 2019–2020 earnings upside justifies the multiple and more, with the base case nearer $24–26. If it slips, or Intel prices aggressively while AMD's ~37% gross margin — barely half of Intel's 60%+ — leaves no cushion to fight back, a de-rate toward 20x 2019 EPS implies roughly $13–14, about 25–30% below the current price. Known near-term drags (crypto-driven GPU channel volatility, second-half seasonality) add noise to an already demanding setup.

That asymmetry — solid but not extreme upside against real execution and competitive-response risk — is the definition of a hold, not a sell: the structural story argues against exiting, but paying 28x forward earnings for a plan that must execute flawlessly argues against buying here. Add on evidence, not on momentum: EPYC design wins converting into disclosed server revenue, gross-margin guidance above ~38%, or a pullback that restores the margin of safety.

One-line justification: Hold — at ~$19 and ~28x 2019 EPS, the 2%→5% server-share story is already in the price, so the risk/reward favors waiting for a better entry or proof the ramp is real.
