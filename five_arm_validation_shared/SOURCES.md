  licensed papers (PubMed Central / PMC). Most PMC papers are CC BY —
  attribution given in each case's GT titles map (results/domain-keys/).
  Before public redistribution, each cited paper's licence was checked:
  CC BY permits derivative educational use with attribution.

  results/finance-regrade-outcome-based.md for why and how.

  (original content, no external sourcing).
- Legal (02-legal): public court-record history (cold-case and landmark
  opinions); no proprietary text reproduced.
- Creative (03-creative): original fiction; no derivative works.
