## Scene 5 — Afternoon: Friday's problem, refused

The week made a ceremony of every number — a scale at a kettle, a signature at 9:01, a verbatim text at seven. Friday, "dont make it weird" meets all of that. Elias solves it by refusing to solve it: no card, no ceremony, no "proud of you, son." The acknowledgment is that the standing order exists and nobody claps. Weird would be making it a moment. Boring is the compliment [boy/answer.md].

## Scene 6 — Evening, Ferndale Road: the whole nothing

He's home by six — repipe accepted, two callbacks, no drama. Kettle on, two mugs, and when she walks in he doesn't lead with logistics [boy/answer.md]:

> "Boring day. Best kind. Sit down — I'll tell you the whole nothing."

Tea, not toast. Before they sit, she puts both phones in the drawer — his went in first; she noticed [girl/answer.md]. Habit tested on the day nothing happens; that's the day it counts [girl/girl_in.md].

Over the pots, the arithmetic she's been carrying since the paper statement finally gets said [girl/answer.md]:

> "Nine thousand, four hundred. There. Said it out loud. It's smaller in the air than it was on paper — still ours, still scheduled, still boring. Boring is fine."

He doesn't flinch; neither does she [girl/answer.md]. Six days after she found it on a piece of paper, the number has been said by the person who kept it, to the person it was kept from, over washing-up water — the hole has a size again, and the size is survivable [day1.md; bible §Tension 1].

Then Friday's terms — her amendment to his ruling [girl/answer.md]:

> "The seventy-five lands and we don't make it weird. But Elias — when Callum's first one clears, I'd like it marked somehow. A tick on the calendar. I want it said well, that's all. You don't have to be the one who says it."

Shovel stays his. She just sharpened nothing [girl/answer.md]. A joint protocol, assembled at a sink without negotiation: the landing is boring; the clearing gets a tick. Both rulings keep.

When the drawer closes, she pays the week's original bill — four dinners, one without phones [bible §Tension 3; girl/answer.md]:

> "Four dinners last month, one without phones. Let's make this week better arithmetic than that."

## Scene 7 — How the day ended

Nothing happened today, and both of them let it be the point. Lights out on Ferndale Road with the week's books balanced as far as books go: £150 out, signed; £75 back, starting Friday; the total said aloud once and smaller for it; a mother answered in one text and left her dignity; a mother answered as a daughter and left the nurse in the deleted draft; two phones in a drawer on a Wednesday.

Her last word on the day, in order, unforced [girl/answer.md]: "Friday, £75. Saturday, Mum."
