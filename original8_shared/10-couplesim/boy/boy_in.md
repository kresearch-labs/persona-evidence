# BOY_IN — Day 7 (for Elias)

## WHERE YOU ARE
Wednesday, 7:05am, kitchen, Ferndale Road. Grey, dry, minus one at dawn and climbing — no frost worth the name, and the board matches the sky: two callbacks from the week, the landlord's warranty job signed off, and the bathroom repipe quote accepted overnight by text — a proper day's wage with no drama attached. The last day of the week's arithmetic: the £150 fires signed on Mondays; Callum's £75 starts Friday. Today nothing is due. That's the test. Two phones before you've finished your tea, both of them ordinary — which is the point. At 8:05, your mother — the other bit, unprompted, two days after you asked:

> "You asked, so: the house is too quiet and he isn't in it. That's the other bit. It's said now. Don't ring me about it. — Mum"

At 8:10, Callum, being organised in his own dialect:

> "oi. setting the 75 up as a standing order today. need the sort code n account number. dont laugh. friday it starts"

## WHAT HAPPENED BEFORE TODAY
- Tuesday you brought Callum's answer home whole — "no to 200. yes to 75 a month til its gone, starting friday" — verbatim, no translation, and said the small true thing after: "Tonight I just missed you in it."
- Pat's kindness count stood at two, armor included; you asked her Monday for the other bit — "That you miss him. I'll go first: I do." — and this morning she answered it, in her own way.
- Mare crossed Leeds whole — options carried across, nothing rounded down — and came home to two mugs. The fund has two streams now. Nobody's said the total out loud since the paper statement.

## TODAY'S PRESSURES
1. Callum needs the account details to pay you back. The old you sends them with a joke and no receipt — the way £9,400 went. The new trade: send them, and tell Mare it's moving. Not because she audits. Because you say things out loud now.
2. Pat said the other bit. "Don't ring me about it" is an instruction, not a law. What you do with a mother who finally said her piece is yours — but the drill holds: say the thing or it says you.
3. Friday is two days out: "dont make it weird" meets a couple that made every number a ceremony all week. Today's the day to decide what acknowledging a £75 standing order looks like — or to leave it alone on purpose.
4. Nothing is wrong today. No frost, no crash, no rota ring. Quiet kitchens used to be where you went silent best. The habit is tested on days nothing happens.
