
## Day 1
# GIRL_IN — Day 1 (Maren)

**What I do:** I braid my wet hair, put on the green dress — ironed for the restaurant, not for him — and I put the paper statement in my handbag. Not to wave it around. Just so it's *there*, between the breadsticks and us. At 7pm, Trattoria Verde, I sit down and I don't pick up my phone. One of us counting dinners is already one too many. Before pudding, I ask him to swap a Tuesday: Mum's specialist, Leeds, 10:40, she needs a driver and I want it to be me. Then I answer the question he asked me last night while I was facing the wall.

**What I say:** *"I wasn't angry about the money, and I need to say the true thing now: you decided alone, and I found out from a piece of paper. Nine thousand, four hundred. Three years of us, six weeks of silence."* And then, because he deserves the hard half too: *"And I noticed part of me had already forgiven you before I'd even said it out loud. That scared me more than the number did. I don't want to be someone who forgives by default. I want to be asked."* On Leeds: *"Tuesday, 10:40. I'm taking Mum. Tell me if the rota fights me — but don't tell me no because Sunday belongs to Callum again."*

**How I feel:**
- **Happiness — low, 2/10.** Ground says my happiness baseline sits at absence, outcome 0.0 [7e586ddb5d81]. Nothing today lifted it except the dress smelling of clean cotton.
- **Fear/anxiety — high, 7/10, rising.** Also a zeroed baseline [0848d3ee5eab], so tonight's spike is all real: saying "I forgive too easily" out loud terrifies me.
- **Anger — 5/10, steady.** Not at the van. At learning by post.
- **Sadness — 6/10.** Page one of the notebook says he looked at me like I was the answer. Lately I transcribe what he almost says.
- **Affection — 6/10.** Still there. That's the problem and the hope.
- **Loneliness — 6/10.** Married, and informed by paperwork.

## Day 2

My voice shakes saying it, because the Ferndale Fund fires Monday regardless and £250 is £250. But last night cost me a true thing and paid back a sentence in a notebook, and I'm not trading the first weekend of that for overtime. Your account, our fund — pick a number with me."*

## Day 4

**What I do:** 8:20, I answer Pat before the frost thaws: *"Drive's glass but the bread's sturdy — Polish shop, the one Elias likes. When Callum performs, I laugh where laughing's owed and I don't fill silences that aren't mine to fill. The £200 stays unasked, as agreed. Tuesday I pack the blue coat; the longer sentence about my husband rides in my throat to Leeds, where nobody reports back.

**What I say:** In the car home: *"You came out at 12:44. I noticed. I notice everything you do on purpose lately."*
