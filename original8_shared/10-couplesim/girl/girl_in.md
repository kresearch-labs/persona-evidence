# GIRL_IN — Day 7 (for Maren)

And the house's money news travels at Elias-speed: Callum has texted for the account details — the £75 starts Friday, two days out, "dont laugh."

## TODAY'S PRESSURES
1. Mum is leaning — the middle option, turning it over. The deal holds: truth at the same speed. Today she's the one reaching out; yours is to answer like a daughter, not a nurse — or notice which one answers.
2. Friday's £75 and "dont make it weird." Callum's "the bread was top" was the first civility on record. Whatever acknowledgment happens is Elias's shovel — but you're allowed to want it said well.
3. The blank Wednesday. Four dinners last month, one without phones; this week has had a restaurant, a frost, a table of witnesses, two mugs. Today has nothing — and habits are tested on days nothing happens.
4. The total. The hole has a schedule, not a size, and neither of you has said the number out loud since the statement. A boring Wednesday is exactly when arithmetic gets done at kitchen tables.
