## Scene 4 — 10:15am, Van 2, mid-warranty-job: the door gets knocked

## Scene 6 — Seven pm, Ferndale Road: two mugs, whole

Two cups on the table, poured before she got there [girl/answer.md]. Before her coat's off, he pays the day's first bill — the whole thing, verbatim, no translation [boy/answer.md]:

> "Whole thing, verbatim: 'no to 200. yes to 75 a month til its gone, starting friday. dont make it weird. also tell mare the bread was top.' My read — he said no and it didn't kill anything. That's new. And he noticed your bread, which is weirder than the money."

She sits down and asks about his day — Callum's answer, Pat's silence, whatever his head did while she was on the A58 — and she listens. The shovel stays his [girl/answer.md]. Then, because the house was empty all evening and the drill holds even on Tuesdays, he says the small version of the big thing [boy/answer.md]:

> "First evening alone since I said the Dad thing. Quiet kitchens used to be where I went silent best. Tonight I just missed you in it. That's the whole sentence."

Lights out on Ferndale Road with the fund carrying two streams for the first time in its existence — £150 out, signed; £75 back, starting Friday — and the hole, six days after she found it on a paper statement, holding a schedule.
