8|## 1. VISION (game_director seat)
9|
10|**One line: The job is the story.**
11|
12|You are the last keeper of a storm-ravaged island lighthouse. The keepers before you
13|vanished, one by one. The supply boat stopped coming. Every night a storm tests the
14|light; every day the island shows you what last night cost — and what the keepers
15|before you left behind.
16|
17|Pillars (in priority order — when in doubt, cut toward the top):
18|1. **Every duty has mechanical weight.** Nothing is decorative. If a duty doesn't
19|   change a number that matters, it doesn't ship.
20|2. **The island remembers.** Wrecks, damage, erosion, reefs — the island is the
21|   history of your playthrough, physically.
22|3. **The record is the story.** No dialogue choices. An auditable log of duties done
23|   and skipped decides what the inspector reads and which ending you earn.
24|4. **The storm is the antagonist.** Procedural, readable, fair. It has tells. It
25|   never cheats.
26|
27|Tone: quiet dread + competence fantasy. The Papers, Please precedent: mechanics carry
28|the meaning; you live in the world your decisions produced.
29|
30|Name-promise check [game_director:76c0c46d67f3]: "The Keeper's Ledger" promises a
31|keeper's job AND a ledger that matters. The build delivers exactly these two things.
32|Feature creep is the enemy; anything not in this doc is out of scope.

34|## 2. CORE LOOP (gamedesigner seat)
35|
36|**Cycle = one Day + one Night. Target 12–18 real minutes. A full run = 12 cycles + ending (~3h).**
37|
38|### DAY (work phase)
39|1. **Read the night's report** at the log desk: what the beam did, which ships passed
40|   or were lost, what failed, what washed ashore.
41|2. **Duties** (each maps to mechanics — see section 3):
42|   - Load oil / set the ration
43|   - Clean the lens (clarity), repair the mechanism (rotation + wick feed)
44|   - Salvage wrecks on Wreck Shore (parts, oil)
45|   - Report wrecks at the chart house (marks them on the chart, prevents reefs)
46|   - Maintain paths (drainage) where storms eroded them
47|   - Keep the log (write the day's entry — feeds the record's completeness)
48|3. **Explore** for the five traces of the previous keepers (optional, story-critical).
49|4. **Light the lamp at dusk.** The night then runs on what you actually loaded, set,
50|   cleaned, and repaired. No take-backs.
51|
52|### NIGHT (storm phase)
53|- A procedural storm rolls in (section 4). The beam runs autonomously from your day's work.
54|- You may move through the tower at night: trim the wick, nudge the ration, restart a
55|  stalled mechanism — physical interventions under time pressure, not menus.
56|- Ships spawn on lanes and resolve against your beam's real reach, the real fog, and
57|  the real hazard map (including reefs your unreported wrecks became).
58|- Outcomes post to the ledger at dawn: PASSED / TURNED AWAY / WRECKED.
59|
60|### DAWN
61|Results post. Damage persists. New wrecks may have washed ashore. Autosave (diegetic:
62|"the log is written"). The island shows its memory.

64|## 3. THE JOB-MATTERS SYSTEMS (anti-decoration law — the heart of the game)
65|
66|### 3.1 OIL ECONOMY (game_balance_economist seat)
67|- Oil is the single survival resource. Sources: salvage from wrecks (barrels), a finite
68|  island reserve in the shed, no resupply (the boat is gone — that's the story).
69|- **Burn rate = f(flame height).** You set flame height: higher = longer reach, more burn.
70|- **Ration:** you set the night's ration at the tank. Tank capacity is finite; the
71|  ration decides how long the light holds.
72|- **Dark interval:** if oil hits zero mid-storm, the beam dies for as long as the oil
73|  stays at zero. Every ship in fog during a dark interval is at maximum risk. The
74|  record logs the interval to the minute.
75|- Tradeoff: burn bright (save ships, drain reserve) vs ration (risk dark intervals).
76|  Storm fury is random enough that neither pure strategy dominates [game_balance_economist:0b5d7f574911].
77|
78|### 3.2 LENS → BEAM REACH
79|- **Clarity 0–100%:** salt crust and soot accumulate daily; cleaning restores it.
80|  Clarity multiplies effective reach, and fog is the storm's main weapon — so lens
81|  work is literally ships-saved.
82|- **Alignment:** mechanism wear degrades alignment; repair restores it.
83|- **Cracks:** neglect clarity below 20% for two nights and the lens cracks — a
84|  permanent reach penalty until repaired with lens parts salvaged from wrecks.
85|
86|### 3.3 MECHANISM (rotation + wick feed)
87|- The lens assembly rotates; the flash characteristic is how ships identify the light.
88|  Wear → rotation stalls → steady beam → ships misread it in rain.
89|- Wick feed jams → flame dips → dark intervals even with oil in the tank.
90|- Both repaired with clockwork/gears salvaged from wrecks. Wear is visible (wobble,
91|  tick irregularity — audio tells before failure).
92|
93|### 3.4 WRECKS → REEFS (the remembering island)
94|- Storms wash wrecks ashore (hull sections, cargo, oil barrels, sometimes a crew's
95|  belongings — story material).
96|- Each wreck must be **salvaged** (parts + oil) AND **reported** at the chart house.
97|- **Unreported wreck = unmarked hazard.** Later storms push it off the shore into the
98|  approach lanes; it becomes a reef that wrecks future ships. The island literally
99|  accumulates your neglect as new dangers on the map.
100|- Reported wrecks get a buoy (built from salvage) — marked, safe, visible.
101|- Island holds max 6 wreck sites. Each unreported one raises ship-loss odds in every
102|  later storm. This is the game's memory made physical.
103|
104|### 3.5 THE KEEPER RECORD (auditable log — the story spine)
105|- Every duty done or skipped posts a timestamped, categorized entry:
106|  LAMP / LENS / MECHANISM / WRECKS / LOG / ISLAND.
107|- Entries are **facts, not judgments**: "Night 4: dark interval 47 min. 2 ships unaccounted."
108|- The record is a real serialized data structure (in the save file), viewable in-game
109|  as the ledger book.
110|- **The inspector reads it** at the end (section 6). The ending is arithmetic on the
111|  record — no hidden flags, no dialogue.
112|- **The five traces** (previous keepers) each show THEIR record fragment: what they
113|  neglected, what it cost. The mystery assembles from records — yours and theirs.
114|  The truth: each keeper broke under the same job; the sea took them when their
115|  records failed. No monsters. The horror is the ledger.
116|- **Story-emerges-from-sim guarantee:** trace texts reference duty categories and
117|  consequences, never player-specific state they can't know — so the story cannot
118|  contradict the sim, because it is made of the sim's own data.
119|
120|### 3.6 SHIPS (the score)
121|- Ships spawn on 2–3 approach lanes per night; count scales with fury and cycle.
122|- Resolution per ship: sees the beam (reach × clarity × alignment vs fog) → holds a
123|  safe course; else risk roll against hazards (rocks, unreported reefs, drifting wrecks).
124|- Outcomes: PASSED / TURNED AWAY (safe, no credit) / WRECKED (distress flare at night;
125|  the wreck washes ashore next day — and waits for your report).
126|- You never talk to ships. Flares, bells, and the beam are the only voices.
127|
128|## 4. STORM SYSTEM (procedural)
129|- Parameters: fury 1–5, wind direction/speed, rain density, fog density, duration,
130|  gust events, lightning strikes.
131|- **Seeded:** storm seed = run_seed + cycle → deterministic per run (reproducible for
132|  QA and for the critic rubric).
133|- Fury curve across 12 cycles (escalation with breather valleys [game_level_content_designer:bb3570f4e15b]):
134|  `1, 2, 2, 3, 2, 4, 3, 4, 5, 3, 5, 5` — tuned by balance seat; breathers before spikes.
135|- Gusts above threshold damage the mechanism, crack the lens path, erode paths.
136|- Lightning = flash light + synthesized thunder. **No VolumetricCloud actors** (Step 1
137|  budget law: 15ms GPU). Storm sky = volumetric fog + SkyAtmosphere + rain particles.

139|## 5. ISLAND LAYOUT (game_level_content_designer seat)
140|One island, five regions, walkable end-to-end in ~4–6 minutes. No fast travel (the
141|walk is short and the island IS the game).
142|
143|1. **Lighthouse Point** (center-south, high ground): the tower — lamp room (lens,
144|   mechanism, tank), keeper's room below (log desk, record book, bed).
145|2. **Cottage Row** (east): cottage, supply shed (finite starting stock), signal post.
146|3. **Wreck Shore** (west): where the sea returns what it takes; salvage bench.
147|4. **Old Landing** (north): the pier, the supply boat's last mooring (story), the
148|   chart house (wreck chart + report desk).
149|5. **The Back Hills** (south): the five trace sites of the vanished keepers, hidden in
150|   weather and terrain; a spring; the old path.
151|
152|**Paths erode:** storm gusts damage the main path (mud, rockfall). Eroded path = slow
153|travel until you maintain it (drainage duty). The island's geography carries your
154|history [game_level_content_designer:bb3570f4e15b].
155|
156|**Secret density:** traces reward exploration, never gate the core loop
157|[game_level_content_designer:88b0470741a8]. You can finish all 12 cycles without
158|finding a single trace — and get a worse ending for it, honestly earned.
159|
160|**Late-game anti-grind:** cycles 9–12 mutate the job with what the island has become
161|(new reefs you made or marked, eroded or maintained paths, wreck sites open or
162|cleared) — the late game is your history demanding attention, not the same loop
163|harder [game_level_content_designer:c811113b62ec — avoid repetitive grind samey].

248|## 12. BALANCE SHEET (game_balance_economist seat — the duty economy)
249|Numbers v1 (tune in playtests; every change logged in section 17):
250|
251|- **Oil:** tank 100 units. Burn = flame_height(0.5–1.5) × 6 units/hour-of-night.
252|  Night = 8 game-hours. Full flame full night = 48 units. Ration caps the night's burn.
253|- **Oil sources:** wreck barrels 15–25 each (1–2 per storm); shed reserve 120 total
254|  (finite — the clock of the run); no other source. Total economy ≈ 120 + ~10 barrels
255|  ≈ enough for ~26 night-equivalents at full flame — scarcity is real by cycle 8+.
256|- **Lens clarity:** decays 8–15%/day (storm-dependent). Cleaning = 1 day-duty, full restore.
257|  Reach multiplier = 0.4 + 0.6×clarity. Crack penalty −25% until repaired (2 lens parts).
258|- **Mechanism:** wear +5–12%/night (gust-dependent). Stall risk = wear% above 60.
259|  Repair = 1 day-duty + 1 clockwork part.
260|- **Wrecks:** 0–2 wash ashore per storm. Salvage = 1 duty each. Report = 1 duty each.
261|  Unreported wreck: +8% ship-loss odds per storm per wreck (compounding, max 6 wrecks).
262|- **Ships:** 2–5 per night by fury. Base loss odds 12% in fury-3 fog with healthy beam;
263|  scales with (fog × (1−reach) × hazards).
264|- **Paths:** erosion events per storm; eroded path = 2× travel time until maintained.
265|- **Traces:** 5 total; each requires reaching its site (weather-gated, not skill-gated).
266|- **No dominant strategy check** [game_balance_economist:0b5d7f574911]: full-flame
267|  heroics drains the reserve by cycle ~9 (dark intervals later); pure rationing
268|  accumulates loss odds via unreported wrecks and lens cracks. The counter to both
269|  is the wreck economy — salvage is the only renewable, and it requires reporting.
270|- **Weakest-option tracking** [game_balance_economist:2770c06f838f]: if playtesting
271|  shows a duty nobody takes (e.g. path maintenance), it gets buffed or cut publicly
272|  in the changelog — no zombie duties.
273|- **Readable tells** [game_balance_economist:694d025cc82f]: every failure state has a
274|  pre-failure signal (mechanism tick irregularity, lens salt shimmer, tank gauge,
275|  weather glass at the chart house).
