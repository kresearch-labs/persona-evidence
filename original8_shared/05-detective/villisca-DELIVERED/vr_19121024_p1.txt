Mr. Oscar Woertendyke of Porter-
ville, California, and Miss Laura Hell­
er of Nodaway were united in mar­
riage Monday evening. October 21,
1912, at half past eight o'clock. The
wedding was the result of a twelve
years courtship and the ceremony wa
performed by Rdv. Koser at the M.
E. parsonage in Nodaway. Mr. Woer;t-
endyke"returned to Nodaway about
a -month ago and has been visiting
friends in that vicinity since that
time. Mr. and Mrs. Woertendyke left
Tuesday for Creston where they will
visit until Thursday of this week.
They will then return to Nodaway for
a short visit before leaving for their
home in California. Mr. Woertendyke
is a painter and decorater. Both he
and his bride are well known and
highly respected in Nodaway, where
they have the best wishes of their
many friends for a happy and pros
perous future.

County Superintendent Clara L
Cowgill has effected the organiza­
tion of a number township teach­
ers' associations in Montgomery coun
ty, and the first of the East town­
ship association's meeting was held
in room 4 at the high school building
in this city last Friday afternoon.
Miss Ruth Means is chairman of the
East township committee, but was
unable to bo present at the meeting
last Friday. After some discussion
of the movement it was decided to
take up the reading of Strayer's "A
Brief Course in the Teaching Pro­
cess," and they will read the first
three chapters at their next month­
ly meeting.
Those who were present last Fri­
day were: Misses Ruth Moore, Ru­
by Ireland, Pearl Dennis, Mary Mill­
er and Fannie Mathews. The meet­
ings are held on Friday, so that no
time will be taken from the teachers
on Saturday.
