TEMPORARY STREET LIGHTS
Boara or" Directors of Commercial)
Club Negotiates With W. M. Bink-
ley for Temporary Street Lighting
Arrangement
Acting upon call of the president, J.
L. Smith, the board of directors of the
Villisca Commercial Club has entered
into negotions with \V. M. Ttinkley, of
the Villisca Public Service Co. to pro­
vide a temporary street lighting ser­
vice in Villisca to begin immediately
and endure until August first and
longer unless terminated by either the.
Commercial Club or the Light Co. By
the terms of a contract signed by Mr.
Binkley and an authorized committee
from the Commercial Club there are
to be ninety, 40-watt lamps to burn
all night at a maximum cost of §1.25
per lamp per month.
The action of the board of directors
of the Commercial Club is not meant
to embarass the city council in their
fight for a better lighting service, and
it is the distinct understanding by all
parties to the agreement that the ar­
rangement shall in no wise be used to
prejudice the city's case in court. The
commercial club is not seeking to take
any side in the light controversey but
has taken this step purely in an effort
to provide lights until sueh time as the
council can act. It is desired that there
be lights in the residence portions of
the city at any rate.

ca and Mrs. Glendora Norton were
married at the home of Mrs G. C. Pot-
teu at St. Joseph Tuesday evening of
this week afc nine o'clock. The cere­
