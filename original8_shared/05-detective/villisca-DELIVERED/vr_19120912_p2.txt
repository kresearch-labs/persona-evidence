Some Of The Good Things
On The Program
The executive committee of the Old Settlers'
Association has arranged an unusually good Pro­
gram for Old Settlers' Day in Villisca this year,
and some of the good things are here listed.
The speaker of the day will be
Senator W. S. Kenyon
one of the ablest platform men in the United States,
and a most popular man with the people. To hear
him alone will be worth coming many miles.
Other events scheduled are: Free band con­
certs, open air entertainments, vocal music, a fid­
dler's contest, a ball game, free moving pictures in
the park, big basket dinner, and other attractions.
Don't forget the date.
