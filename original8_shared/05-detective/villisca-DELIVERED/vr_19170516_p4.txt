That sounds like a strange statement, but there

A shortage has continued, now, for two years

The shortage may not be local, or sectional, or
seasonal, but it is national, universal and con­
tinuous.
Unlimited production is not consistent with
Dodge Brothers scrupulous methods of manu­
facture.
They must always give to their car that over-
care which the public expects from them.
It is the root, and the source, and the reason for
that steadiness of demand we are describing.
They will never go beyond a production point
which might place their reputation in jeopardy.
A more remarkable recognition of merit on the
part of the public it would be difficult to con­
ceive.

absorbed in an increased market.

grown till they are almost a city( in'themselves.

to be in sight, the horizon of that market widens'
and draws itself still farther away. ' '

almost absurd.
That simple reason is that the goodness of the
car is seldom called into question.
The thoughts about it are the same thoughts
thousands of minds.

sale and the use of practically every car that
leaves the works.

ers in the world.
But they will never imperil their production by
sacrificing quality to mere quantity.
For that reason we believe the condition will al­
ways be what it is now.
We believe the goodness of the car will always
produce a demand it will be impossible to entire­
