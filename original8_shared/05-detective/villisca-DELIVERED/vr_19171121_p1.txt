Carl Faye Elliott, son of Mr.
and Mrs. H. F. Elliott of this city, and
Miss Helen Taylor, daughter of Mr.
and Mrs. J.F. Taylor of Morton Mills,
were united in marriage Sunday morn­
ing at about nine o'clock at the local
Methodist parsonage. Rev. E S. Men-
oher said the ceremony. The bride
and groom were accompanied -by J
Li. Wheeler of this city.
The bride and groom of this occas
ion are well and favorably known to
many of the people of Villisca and
vicinity. The groom is an energetic
clerk at the Rexall drug store in this
city and there and at other positions
in this city he has made a large host
of friends. H e attended the Villisca
high school for two years and then
accepted the position at the drug
store.
The bride also attended the Villisca
high school and graduated in the class
of 1916. Following her graduation she
spent one year as teacher at the Mor­
ton Mills school, one year teaching
the Neal school southwest of Villisca
and the past school term has taught
at the Mayhew school eight miles
northwest of this city. She was born
and h a s been reared in Villisca and
has a wide circle of friends and ac­
quaintances in this community.
Friends of both parties Friday even­
ing of last week showed thoughtful-
ness for the two parties in a prenup
tial miscellaneous shower given at the
O V. Wise home in this city. There
were'a number of relatives and friends
present on that occasion and the
young people were recipients of many
beautiful and useful articles for their
new home.
They will be at home at once to
their friends in the Dr. C. J. Graf
residence on East Fifth street in this
city.
The Review joins the many friends
of Mr. and Mrs. Elliott in extending
to them congratulations and best
wishes for a happy married life

The Misses Julia and Teckla Eden,

Maude Roberts and Opal Day

decided to continue t o "Villisca. It
was while descending t h e Davie hill

Eden w a s also driving west
The collision threw Mr. Eden onto
the heels of h i s team and threw the
girls from t h e buggy. The Eden team
ran with t h e buggy to the street near
the Mrs. Sue Overman home and were
there found by Mr. Eden and Albert

and Teckla Eden were most injured

All three daughters and Mr. Eden were
