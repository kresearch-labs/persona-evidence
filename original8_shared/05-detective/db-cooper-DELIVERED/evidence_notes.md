## 3. The black J.C. Penney tie and tie clip  (build-up)

SOURCE 3.1 — FBI case history page (see 0.1)
- Verbatim (image caption): "During the hijacking, Cooper was wearing this black J.C. Penney tie, which he removed before jumping; it later provided us with a DNA sample."
- Supports: tie was left behind; FBI says it yielded a DNA sample. Does NOT say the DNA profile was full/usable for identification.

SOURCE 3.2 — Citizen Sleuths [INDEPENDENT RESEARCH GROUP], "Overview of 'Snap-on' Tie from Penneys"
- URL: https://www.citizensleuths.com/overview-of-snap-on-tie-from-penneys
- Verbatim: "All of the particles from the tie shown in this research are too small for the human eye to see directly and are much smaller than even a particle of dust."
- Verbatim: "The particles identified on the tie represent the only new physical evidence to come along in the D.B. Cooper case since the money find in 1980."
- Supports: particle-scale evidence approach; team's own characterization.

SOURCE 3.3 — Citizen Sleuths, "Physical Evidence" page
- URL: https://www.citizensleuths.com/physical-evidence/
- Verbatim: "Cooper's tie has been stored in the archive since the hijacking in 1971. During the first examination in 2009, multiple sticky tape and scanning electron microscope (SEM) stub samples were lifted from the tie. The focus at that time was to look for pollen that would suggest where in the country Cooper possibly hailed from."
- Verbatim: "Subsequently in 2011 during a return trip to the Archive, the tie was thoroughly sampled using a variety of techniques including, ultra violet light (UV), laser florescence and forensic vacuum for high-density particle collection as outlined in other parts of this study."

SOURCE 3.5 — Citizen Sleuths, "Pollen" page
- URL: https://www.citizensleuths.com/pollen/
- Verbatim: "Fact: The Club Moss spore Lycopodium clavatum is on the tie. Fact: The Lycopodium spores are covered with a powder containing silicon, titanium and alumina. Fact: Silicon, titanium and alumina were used as pill coatings in 1971."
- Verbatim: "Microscopic examination of more than 28 SEM stubs from Cooper's tie revealed a quantity of Lycopodium spores. The Lycopodium spores offer the possibility that Cooper was taking some form of pill during working hours while wearing the tie."
- Supports: Lycopodium spores + pill-coating powder hypothesis (interpretive).

SOURCE 3.6 — Citizen Sleuths, "Titanium Particles from Cooper's Tie"
- URL: https://www.citizensleuths.com/titanium-particles-from-coopers-tie/
- Verbatim: "The large particle in green has a spectrum that shows it is pure titanium metal, while the smaller attached particle in red is a form of stainless steel."
- Verbatim: "The fact that military or commercial aircraft all use alloyed titanium exclusively, ruled them out since the particles found on Cooper's tie were pure titanium."
- Verbatim: "It was finally determined that the titanium, chlorine and sodium signatures could be found in match heads."
- Verbatim: "The most likely place these factors would come together and produce this type of particle, would be in a fabrication facility using titanium."
- Verbatim: "Two spiral aluminum chips were extracted from the tie (Fig. 6). ... EDS analysis determined that the predominant alloy was magnesium, showing the closest match to either 500 series cast or 5000 series wrought aluminum."
- Supports: pure Ti metal particle + 400-series SS smear; pure-Ti (not alloy) noted; match-head alternative for Na/Cl/Ti signature. Does NOT prove Boeing employment (CS explicitly notes alloyed Ti used in aircraft, pure Ti particles).
- Weakness: "Anecdotal, many particles of brass were also found ... brass screw plugs were used to seal the sample filters" — contamination channel acknowledged.

## 5. Aircraft (N467US, 727) and traces; jump evidence

SOURCE 5.1 — FBI summary report (same, synopsis + Reno search)
- Verbatim: "The plane then took off for Reno and at 8:11 p.m. a pressure oscillation was noted by the plane's crew. That pressure oscillation was probably caused by the hijacker leaving the aircraft. Search of the aircraft at Reno revealed the hijacker was not in the plane when it landed."
- Verbatim: "Also located in sear 18/E of the aircraft at Reno, was a black clip-on tie..." (tie left on seat)
