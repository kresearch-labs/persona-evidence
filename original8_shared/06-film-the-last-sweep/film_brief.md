# FILM BRIEF — "The Last Sweep" (30s animated short)
LEAD: film_narrative_director. Status: LOCKED before any rendering (flow step 1).
Every frame and every sound will be generated by code. Zero stock assets, zero downloads.

## LOGLINE (one sentence)
A small boat guided across a stormy night sea by a distant lighthouse loses the
light — and discovers the glow was always meant to be carried on: the boat begins
to shine, and becomes the lighthouse for the next traveler.

## EMOTION TARGET
**Bittersweet hope** — grief turning into inheritance. Nameable after ONE viewing:
"hope" / "the light carries on". The last 3 seconds decide the emotion
(ground [c1b494eca772], [bc03a26e59da]); the strongest beat lands on the
unexpected frame — the boat, not the lighthouse, is where the light returns
(ground [8111cdfe8516], [75e1021ec3cd]).

## ECHO STRUCTURE (binding contract)
- SETUP SEED (0–8s): one cold light in the upper third sweeps the dark; a small
  triangle boat drifts on the sea; each beam pass makes the boat glimmer.
- TURN (8–22.5s): the storm rises; the sweep falters; at ~16.5s the light dies.
  Longest stillness + ~1.5s of near-silence before the payoff (20.8–22.3s).
- PAYOFF TRANSFORMS THE SEED (22.5–30s): the boat's hull blooms warm from within;
  it raises the SAME beam geometry the lighthouse had; final composition REUSES
  the opening composition, changed — light source now the boat, hue cold→warm,
  and a second tiny triangle approaches from the horizon. The guided becomes the
  guide. (Payoff-reuses-opening ground [4a78b1137647], [e3e9af6c8ccd].)

## ANCHOR FRAME — exactly one
**t ≈ 23.5s (frame ~705)**: the first warm gold blooming inside the hull against
near-black, rain still falling, no beam yet. Visually distinct: the only warm
frame region in the film's first 23 seconds; the only interior-light frame.

## SOUND MAP — 100% synthesized (ground [58205f7d0bce], [396d70654ef4])
- Pad: detuned sine/triangle stack (D2 A2 D3 F3), slow attack, low-passed; opens
  slightly in B2; fades to near-silence in B3.
- SIGNATURE SOUND: the beacon call — two sine notes D5→A4, bell decay, shared
  reverb — played at each beam pass in B1, faltering/detuning in B2.
- Sea: low-passed noise swells amplitude-coupled to the visual noise field.
- Rain: sparse high-passed impulse ticks from B2 onward.
- SILENCE: 20.8–22.3s ≈ 1.5s near-silence (pad out, only rain tail) before payoff.
- TRANSFORMED SIGNATURE (≈26.5s): the beacon call returns — same two notes, warm
  voice (sine + 2nd/3rd harmonics, soft major pad beneath). Same call, warm voice.
- Chain: fx layer (reverb send, eq, width) → st.master() → loudnorm to −14 LUFS
  integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP, zero clipped samples.
